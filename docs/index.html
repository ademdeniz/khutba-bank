Style Guide

Every khutba in this bank follows these house rules. They exist so that any imam or volunteer can pick up a khutba and deliver it with confidence.

  1. Bilingual only. Every content row has a Bosnian cell and an English cell, side by side.
  2. No fabricated hadith or Qur'anic verses. If a citation cannot be verified, it is left out.
  3. Qur'anic Arabic is sourced. Arabic text for every cited verse comes from quran.com or tanzil.net, and every verse citation links to its quran.com page.
  4. Hadith name their collection. Every hadith names its collection (Bukhari, Muslim, Tirmidhi, and so on), checked against sunnah.com.
  5. Keep it short. Reading both languages aloud, Bosnian and English together, takes 10 to 15 minutes at most. That is roughly 1,300 to 1,800 words in total. Each khutba shows its estimated reading time.
  6. Tone. Emotionally grounded, spiritually reflective, warm flowing prose.
  7. No em dashes in the written khutba text. Use commas, full stops, or a new sentence instead.
  8. Sources at the bottom. All sources are cited at the end of each khutba with working links.

Layout of a khutba

  • Arabic verses sit in their own full-width row, right-aligned, directly above the Bosnian and English row that explains them.
  • Khutbas adapted from another source carry the label Adapted and link to the original page.
  • The copy buttons produce plain text, ready to paste into a document or a phone for the minbar.
